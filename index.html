DeskDisplay

DeskDisplay is a personal Windows desktop application that displays Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, and weather information on a connected USB display.

Features

  • Read-only Google Calendar integration
  • Read-only Google Tasks integration
  • Local USB display
  • Personal productivity dashboard

How it works

After you sign in with your Google Account and grant read-only access, DeskDisplay periodically reads your upcoming calendar events and open tasks and draws them on a small display connected to your computer via USB. DeskDisplay never creates, edits, or deletes your calendar events or tasks.

Privacy

Your Google data stays on your computer. Read the DeskDisplay Privacy Policy for details.
