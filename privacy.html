DeskDisplay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

DeskDisplay is a personal Windows desktop application that shows Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, and weather information on a USB display connected to the user's computer. This policy explains what data DeskDisplay accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

1. Access only after your authorization

DeskDisplay accesses your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks only after you sign in with your Google Account and explicitly grant permission on Google's consent screen. Without this authorization, DeskDisplay cannot access any of your Google data.

2. Read-only permissions

DeskDisplay requests only the following read-only permissions:

  • Google Calendar, read-only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly
  • Google Tasks, read-only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.readonly

DeskDisplay cannot create, modify, or delete your calendar events or tasks.

3. What data is used and why

Google Calendar and Google Tasks data is used exclusively to display your personal events and tasks on your local USB display. Specifically, DeskDisplay uses:

  • Google Calendar: the title, start time, and end time of upcoming events in your primary calendar.
  • Google Tasks: task list names, task titles, due dates, and completion status.

This data is refreshed about once per minute and is not used for any other purpose.

4. Where credentials and data are stored

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally, in a file in the DeskDisplay application folder on your computer. They are used only to communicate with Google's servers.

Calendar and task data is processed in your computer's memory. In addition, DeskDisplay keeps the following local files on your computer:

  • an application log file, which may include the title and start time of your next calendar event and the number of your tasks;
  • an image of the current dashboard screen, which contains the event and task information shown on the display.

These files stay on your computer, are not uploaded anywhere, and can be deleted by you at any time.

5. No transfer to developer servers

Google Calendar and Google Tasks data is not transmitted to any server operated by the developer. DeskDisplay has no backend server. The application connects only to:

  • Google's servers, to sign in and to read your calendar events and tasks;
  • the public Open-Meteo weather service, to get the current weather for a fixed location configured in the application.

Weather requests contain only that fixed location and do not contain any of your Google data or other personal information. As with any internet request, the weather service can see your computer's IP address.

6. No selling, no advertising

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. DeskDisplay contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising components.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

DeskDisplay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke DeskDisplay's access to your Google Account at any time in your Google Account security settings, under third-party apps with account access: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

After revoking access, you can remove all locally stored data by deleting the DeskDisplay application folder, including the stored token file, log files, and dashboard image.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
